Know who goes first on Finnish roads

Traffic signs, junctions and roundabouts explained with animated cars. Everyone drives on the right side. Watch it, then try it yourself in the simulator.

01

Stay on the right

In Finland every car drives on the right side of the road. You pass oncoming cars on your left.

  • Stay to the right of the middle line
  • Oncoming cars pass you on your left side
  • Only cross the line to overtake safely
💡The dotted blue path shows where each car goes. Notice both cars hug the right edge.
02

Who goes first? Check in this order

When you reach a junction, ask these four questions from top to bottom. The first one that applies decides.

STEP 1
👮

A police officer or road worker

Hand signals beat everything, even lights and signs.

STEP 2
🚦

Traffic lights

Green means go, red means stop. Lights beat signs.

STEP 3
🛑

Traffic signs

STOP, Give way and Priority road tell you who goes first.

STEP 4
➡️

No signs? Right-hand rule

Let the car coming from your right go first.

🚑Always let emergency vehicles with sirens and blue lights pass, whatever the signs say.
03

The six signs that decide priority

Learn these shapes. Even from far away, the shape tells you what to do: red triangle, red octagon, yellow diamond, blue circle.

04

Junction with no signs: right-hand rule

No signs and no lights? The junction is equal (tasa-arvoinen risteys). Look to your right.

🛑Car A must wait. Car B comes from A’s right side, so B goes first. Watch the “WAIT” bubble above A.
⚠️You may see the Equal junction ahead sign before the junction. It is only a warning, the rule stays the same.
05

Give way and STOP signs

These signs are on the small road. Cars on the main road keep going. It does not matter if they come from your left or your right: you wait for all of them.

Give way: slow down and look

STOP: stop completely first

✅Give way: slow down, and stop only if a car is coming. STOP: always stop fully at the line, even if the road looks empty. Then go when it is safe.
06

Priority road (the yellow diamond)

The yellow diamond means you are on the main road. Side roads must let you pass, so you can drive straight through junctions.

  • Keep going at a safe speed, but still watch for others
  • Side-road cars have a Give way or STOP sign
  • When you see the diamond with a black stripe, the priority road ends
⚠️After the “end of priority road” sign, the next junction is equal again. Use the right-hand rule.
07

Turning left: wait for oncoming cars

When you turn left, you cross the path of cars coming towards you. So you must wait until they have passed. This applies to oncoming cars going straight or turning right.

🚗Car A waits in its lane. Only after Car B drives past does A make the left turn.
08

Roundabouts (kiertoliittymä)

Almost every Finnish roundabout has a Give way sign at each entrance. Cars already inside the circle go first.

  • Drive counter-clockwise around the circle
  • Look to your left before you enter
  • Use your turn signal when you leave
🔄Car A waits at the line until Car B has passed the entrance. Then A joins the circle.
